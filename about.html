About Me

Adetomiwa Tomilola-Joy

GIS Data Analyst — grounded in geology, working in data

I hold a BSc in Geology (Environmental Geology & Geochemistry) from Olabisi Onabanjo University, where my final-year research investigated heavy metal concentration and lateral depth distribution around a dumpsite in the Dahomey Basin, using Inductively Coupled Plasma-Atomic Emission Spectrometry (ICP-AES). That work taught me to collect, clean, and interpret spatial and geochemical data rigorously — a skill set I now apply to GIS and data analytics problems.

Since graduating, i have worked as a content strategist, content writer and freelance research analyst, producing 20+ analytical reports and reporting to stakeholders, while building hands-on data analytics projects in SQL, Power BI, Excel, and learning how to use geospatial tools.

I am currently looking for a role as a Data Analyst — i am someone who already knows how to clean a dataset, question a number, and write up what it means for people who don't have time to dig through it themselves.
